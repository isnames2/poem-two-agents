A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — and what
happens when that power passes between two agents who agree on one plan.
Two voices, one map: the choice was mine, and then it was ours.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network) writes the poem to
`/output/entry.txt`, deterministically — the same bytes on every run.
